## Maker's first comment

> Hi Product Hunt, I'm the maker of Kumquat.
>
> I kept running into the same small chore on my Mac: a file is the wrong format or too big for the place I'm uploading it to, so I open an export dialog, guess a quality setting, check the size, try again. Kumquat is my attempt to make that one gesture.
>
> What's in 1.0:
> - **Shift-drag wheel.** Start dragging a file and tap Shift. Only the actions that make sense for that file fan out around your cursor.
> - **Target size with a quality guard.** "Under 2 MB" shrinks the file and checks that text is still readable. (Pro, and free for your first 10 files)
> - **Finder Quick Actions, Share extension, Shortcuts**, plus a menu bar drop.
> - Originals are never overwritten. Results are saved next to them, and you can undo.
>
> Everything runs on your Mac. There's no Kumquat server, no account and no analytics, ad or crash-reporting SDK. It runs on Intel and Apple silicon Macs with macOS 12 or later, in English and Korean.
>
> Pricing, so there are no surprises: your first 10 files get every feature, Pro included. After that, 5 basic conversions a day are free, and the free version shows ads (a note about Pro or a sponsor we work with directly; no third-party ad SDK, nothing tracked). Pro is $11.99/year with a 7-day free trial, or $24.99 lifetime, $19.99 at launch.
>
> Next on the roadmap: recipes, folder watching and destination presets (1.1), then on-device AI like background removal and OCR (1.2).
>
> I'd love to hear which conversion you do most often and what gets in your way. I'll be here answering comments all day.
