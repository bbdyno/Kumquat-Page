## Maker's first comment

> Hi Product Hunt, I'm the maker of Shrinkwrap.
>
> The chore I kept hitting on my Mac: an email bounces because the attachments are too big, a form says the file is over 5 MB, a chat app rejects a video. I'd open an export dialog, guess a quality setting, check the size and try again. Shrinkwrap starts from the other end: it asks where the file is going.
>
> What's in 1.0:
> - **Destinations.** Email attachment, messenger, small upload, online forms, photo upload, web images or a custom limit. Pick one, drop your files, get new copies that fit. Every limit can be changed in Settings.
> - **Email total budget.** Drop several files and the 20 MB total is split across them by size, so the whole message fits.
> - **Quality guard.** When a limit would crush the quality, it lowers the resolution first, and each result shows its similarity to the original.
> - **PDFs keep their text.** For email and online forms, only the images inside a PDF are compressed.
> - **Send it right away.** Attach results to a new Mail message, share them, or show them in Finder. Files that already fit are left alone.
> - **Anywhere on your Mac.** Main window, menu bar drop, Finder Quick Actions and Shortcuts all start with the destination. Plain format conversion is there too.
>
> Everything runs on your Mac. There's no Shrinkwrap server, no account and no analytics, ad or crash-reporting SDK. It runs on Intel and Apple silicon Macs with macOS 12 or later, in English and Korean.
>
> Pricing, so there are no surprises: your first 10 files get every feature, Pro included. After that, 5 basic conversions a day are free, and the free version shows ads (a note about Pro or a sponsor we work with directly; no third-party ad SDK, nothing tracked). Destinations are Pro: $11.99/year with a 7-day free trial, or $24.99 lifetime, $19.99 at launch.
>
> I'd love to hear where you send files most often and which limits trip you up. I'll be here answering comments all day.
